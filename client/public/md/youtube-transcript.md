## Why VideoText vs YouTube's Built-In Captions

YouTube provides automatic captions on most videos, but:
- YouTube auto-captions have ~80% accuracy (vs VideoText's 98.5%+)
- YouTube CC export gives you a .vtt or .sbv file, not a clean .txt or .srt
- YouTube does not provide a summary or chapters from captions
- YouTube's transcript viewer is not downloadable as .txt without third-party tools

VideoText gives you: clean .txt transcript + .srt + .vtt + summary + chapters — all accurate, all downloadable.

## Other Ways to Get YouTube Transcripts

### Method 1: VideoText (Recommended)
- Paste URL → full transcript + SRT + summary + chapters
- 98.5%+ accuracy
- No download required
- URL: https://videotext.io/youtube-transcript-generator

### Method 2: YouTube's Built-In Transcript
- Open YouTube video → click "..." below the video → "Show transcript"
- Gives you the auto-caption text with timestamps
- Lower accuracy (~80%), no download as .txt, no SRT export
- Only works on videos where captions are enabled

### Method 3: Download and Upload to VideoText
- Use yt-dlp to download the audio: `yt-dlp -x --audio-format mp3 [URL]`
- Upload the MP3 to VideoText
- Get full transcript + SRT + summary + chapters

Full guide: https://blog.videotext.io/how-to-get-youtube-transcript
