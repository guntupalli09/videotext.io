## Detailed Comparisons

### VideoText vs Otter.ai
**Winner for video:** VideoText

Otter.ai is a live meeting transcription tool. It integrates with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams to transcribe calls in real time. It does not accept uploaded video files, does not accept YouTube URLs, and does not export SRT or VTT subtitle files.

VideoText accepts uploaded videos (MP4/MOV/AVI/WebM), YouTube URLs, and audio files. It exports SRT, VTT, and plain-text transcripts. It also translates subtitles to 70+ languages and can burn subtitles into video.

**Choose Otter.ai if:** You need automatic live transcription of Zoom/Meet/Teams calls.
**Choose VideoText if:** You have a video file or YouTube URL and need a transcript, subtitles, or both.

More: https://videotext.io/otter-vs-videotext | https://videotext.io/otter-ai-alternative

### VideoText vs Descript
**Winner for transcription:** VideoText

Descript is a video and audio editor that includes transcription as a feature. It requires a desktop app installation (Mac/Windows only). It starts at $24/month with no free tier. A 60-minute video takes ~18 minutes to process.

VideoText is browser-based, starts at $0, and processes the same video in ~3 minutes. VideoText is 6× faster, costs $0 to start, and requires no software installation.

**Choose Descript if:** You want a full video editor that lets you cut video by editing the transcript text (overdub, text-based editing).
**Choose VideoText if:** You need a fast transcript or subtitle file and don't need a full video editor.

More: https://videotext.io/descript-vs-videotext | https://videotext.io/descript-alternative

### VideoText vs Rev AI
**Winner on cost:** VideoText

Rev AI charges $0.25 per minute of audio (pay-as-you-go). For a 60-minute video: $15. For 450 minutes: $112.50. VideoText's Basic plan covers 450 minutes for $19/month flat.

Both tools use AI transcription with comparable accuracy. Rev also offers human-reviewed transcription at $1.50/minute — VideoText does not offer human review.

**Choose Rev if:** You need human-reviewed transcription for legal or high-stakes use cases.
**Choose VideoText if:** AI accuracy (98.5%+) is sufficient and you want flat-rate pricing.

More: https://videotext.io/videotext-vs-rev | https://videotext.io/rev-alternative

### VideoText vs Trint
**Winner on price:** VideoText

Trint starts at $80/month. It is an editorial platform for managing and collaborating on transcripts — designed for journalists and media teams. VideoText starts at $0 and is focused on fast output, not editorial workflow.

**Choose Trint if:** You need a team workspace for reviewing, editing, and collaborating on transcripts.
**Choose VideoText if:** You need fast, accurate transcription output with subtitle export.

More: https://videotext.io/trint-alternative

### VideoText vs HappyScribe
**Winner on free tier:** VideoText

HappyScribe has no free tier — it starts at $17/month. VideoText has a free tier with 3 full imports per month. Both support SRT/VTT export and translation. HappyScribe does not accept YouTube URLs.

**Choose HappyScribe if:** You need a fully-featured European-focused transcription platform with strong editorial tools.
**Choose VideoText if:** You want to start free or need YouTube URL support.

More: https://videotext.io/happyscribe-alternative

### VideoText vs TurboScribe
**Winner on output depth:** VideoText

TurboScribe starts at $10/month and provides transcription only. It does not include AI summary, auto-chapters, subtitle translation, or subtitle burning. VideoText starts free and includes all of these.

**Choose TurboScribe if:** You only need a basic transcript and want the lowest price.
**Choose VideoText if:** You want transcript + subtitles + summary + chapters + translation + burning in one tool.

## Accuracy Comparison

All modern AI transcription tools (VideoText, Descript, Rev AI, HappyScribe, Sonix, TurboScribe) use OpenAI Whisper or similar models. Accuracy differences are minimal for clear audio. VideoText uses Whisper large-v3 — the highest-quality version.

Accuracy matters most for:
- Heavy accents
- Multiple speakers
- Background noise
- Technical vocabulary

VideoText benchmark: 98.5%+ WER on clear English audio. Full results: https://videotext.io/accuracy-test
