## What Is Video Captioning?

Video captioning is the process of generating time-synchronized text (captions or subtitles) from a video's audio track. Captions improve accessibility, boost SEO, increase engagement on social media, and are required for many platforms.

Two types:
- **Open captions (burned-in):** Permanently embedded in the video frame. Cannot be turned off. Best for social media.
- **Closed captions (SRT/VTT files):** Separate text file uploaded alongside the video. Can be toggled. Best for YouTube and LMS platforms.

VideoText supports both.

## Caption Quality Standards

VideoText captions meet these broadcast standards:
- **Netflix:** ≤42 chars/line, ≤17 CPS, ≤2 lines
- **YouTube:** ≤80 chars/line, ≤21 CPS
- **BBC:** ≤37 chars/line, ≤17 CPS
- **EBU R37:** ≤21 CPS
