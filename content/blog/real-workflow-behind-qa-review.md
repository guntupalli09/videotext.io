# Transcript QA Process: The 7-Step Review Workflow That Passes Grading

**Transcript QA** (searched as **transcript qa**, **qa transcription**, and **qa process annotation**) is a structured sequence — not “read through and fix things.” Platforms like Rev reject on formatting and tags even when words are 97% accurate.

> **Run QA on pre-formatted output:** [Transcribe →](https://videotext.io/video-to-transcript) then [apply client guidelines before the QA pass →](https://videotext.io/guideline-format). Verification beats discovery.

---

## Why a single QA pass fails

A combined read asks you to simultaneously:

- Verify content vs. audio  
- Check speaker attribution  
- Apply formatting rules  
- Validate tags and timestamps  
- Fix paragraph structure  

Each mode competes for attention. Combined-pass reviewers catch **content** errors well and **formatting** errors poorly — yet formatting triggers most marketplace rejections.

---

## Step 1: Client requirements re-read (2 min)

Re-read the job brief **before** QA starts. Working memory does not survive a 2-hour session.

Confirm: verbatim mode, speaker label format, timestamp rules, glossary terms, inaudible/cross-talk tag specs.

---

## Step 2: Structural integrity (read-only)

- Timestamp coverage at required intervals  
- Speaker label consistency (find every variant)  
- Word count vs. audio length (~8k–9k words / 60 min)  
- Paragraph breaks at speaker changes  

Fix bulk structure **before** line-level review.

**Time:** 5–10 min / 30 min audio.

---

## Step 3: Tag compliance pass

Search and bulk-fix:

- `[inaudible]` lowercase, bracketed — not `[Inaudible]`, `[unclear]`, or blank  
- `[crosstalk]` per platform spec  
- Non-verbals: `[laughs]`, `[applause]` — exact format  

Graders check tags. This step is fast and high-leverage.

**Time:** 3–5 min / 30 min audio.

---

## Step 4: Glossary and proper noun pass

List expected proper nouns → search each → batch-correct phonetic substitutions.

AI errors cluster: same wrong word every time. Find-and-replace beats twelve individual listen fixes.

**Time:** 5–10 min / 30 min audio.

---

## Step 5: Formatting and style compliance

Against the style guide, verify:

- Numbers (spell 1–10, digits 11+)  
- Punctuation for clean vs full verbatim  
- Speaker label exact format  
- Timestamp sample checks across the doc  

**Time:** 8–15 min / 30 min audio.

---

## Step 6: Targeted listen pass

Audio only for:

- Flagged uncertain words  
- Unverified proper nouns  
- Noisy / cross-talk segments  
- Structural red flags from Step 2  

**Not** full linear playback on clean AI output.

**Time:** 0.4x–0.6x audio length.

---

## Step 7: Final edit pass

Fix all flags in one sweep. Re-read first and last paragraph — endings get the least attention and the most grader scrutiny.

## FAQ: Transcript QA

**What is transcript QA?**  
The structured review after transcription: accuracy, formatting, tags, speakers, and client-rule compliance. See also: [What is transcript QA? →](https://videotext.io/blog/what-is-transcript-qa)

**Why do AI transcripts fail QA?**  
Accuracy is often fine; style rules, proper nouns, and speaker labels fail. [Why AI transcripts fail QA →](https://videotext.io/blog/why-ai-transcripts-fail-qa)

**How is QA different from transcript cleanup?**  
Cleanup is the transcriptionist’s edit pass. QA is the verification layer — often by a second reviewer or grader. [Transcript cleanup workflow →](https://videotext.io/blog/how-professional-transcriptionists-clean-transcripts)
