# What Is Transcript QA? Checklist + Tools

**What is transcript QA?** It is the structured review pass **after** transcription — separate from typing or AI capture. Searches for **transcript qa** and **qa transcription** spike when a deliverable gets rejected for “failure to meet customer notes.”

> **Cut QA time in half:** [Transcribe with AI →](https://videotext.io/video-to-transcript) then [pre-apply client style rules →](https://videotext.io/guideline-format) so QA becomes verification, not discovery.

## Transcript QA vs transcription vs cleanup

| Phase | Who | Goal |
|-------|-----|------|
| **Transcription** | AI or typist | Words on page |
| **Transcript cleanup** | Transcriptionist | Accuracy + rough formatting | [Cleanup guide →](https://videotext.io/blog/how-professional-transcriptionists-clean-transcripts) |
| **Transcript QA** | Reviewer / grader | Client-rule compliance before delivery | This post |

QA merges **objective checks** (timestamp drift, tag schema) with **subjective readability** (verbatim mode, paragraph flow).

## Minimum transcript QA checklist

1. **Proper nouns** — cross-check order-form glossary; batch-fix phonetic substitutions  
2. **Speaker formatting** — labels match screenshot samples exactly (colon, dash, capitalization)  
3. **Uncertain audio** — mandated tags (`[inaudible]`) instead of guesses  
4. **Audio holes** — blank lines or bracket text per client PDF  
5. **Verbatim mode** — clean vs full verbatim applied consistently | [Clean vs full verbatim →](https://videotext.io/blog/clean-verbatim-vs-full-verbatim)  
6. **Timestamps** — format, placement, no drift on long files | [Fix subtitle timing →](https://videotext.io/fix-subtitles)  
7. **Tag compliance** — `[inaudible]`, `[crosstalk]`, non-verbals per platform spec  

Print this list. Run it in order. [Full 7-step QA workflow →](https://videotext.io/blog/real-workflow-behind-qa-review)

## Why AI makes transcript QA harder, not easier

AI accuracy on clean audio is often 95%+. **QA failure rates did not drop proportionally** because:

- AI does not know client-specific rules  
- Proper nouns fail silently (look like real words)  
- Speaker diarization breaks on cross-talk  
- No uncertainty flags — reviewers must treat the whole doc as suspect

## FAQ

**What does QA stand for in transcription?**  
Quality assurance — the final review before a transcript is client-ready.

**Is transcript QA the same as proofreading?**  
No. Proofreading is language-only. Transcript QA includes timestamps, speakers, tags, and platform-specific formatting.

**How long does transcript QA take?**  
~45–51 minutes per 30 minutes of audio with structured passes on 94%+ AI output. [Time benchmarks →](https://videotext.io/blog/real-workflow-behind-qa-review)
