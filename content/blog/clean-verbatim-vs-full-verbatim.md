# Clean Verbatim vs Full Verbatim Transcription

**Clean verbatim transcription** keeps meaning-first readability — fillers and obvious stumbles go away unless they matter legally or narratively. **Full verbatim** traps every audible disfluency: false starts, repeated words, `[um]`, trailing dashes. Reviewers reject work when you pick the wrong mode.

> **Apply the right verbatim preset before delivery:** [Format → Client guidelines →](https://videotext.io/guideline-format) Rev, GoTranscript, and custom PDF rules as editable cards.

---

## Quick comparison

| | Clean verbatim | Full verbatim |
|---|----------------|---------------|
| Fillers (`um`, `uh`, `like`) | Removed | Kept |
| False starts | Usually tightened | Both attempts kept |
| Readability | High | Legal / research fidelity |
| Common on | Podcasts, general media | Legal, academic, strict client PDFs |
| Search term | **clean verbatim transcription** | full verbatim transcription |

## Clean verbatim transcription — examples

**Audio:** “So, um, I think we should, you know, move forward with the, the acquisition.”

**Clean verbatim output:**  
“So I think we should move forward with the acquisition.”

**Full verbatim output:**  
“So, um, I think we should, you know, move forward with the, the acquisition.”

One wrong choice → rejection on Rev, GoTranscript, or agency QA.

---

## Signals to resolve before QC

- Filler ladders (“like,” “you know”) — remove or retain?  
- False starts that finish later — mirror both or tighten?  
- Laughter / environmental sounds — newline, brackets, timestamps?  
- Digits spelled out vs normalized numerals?  

These are not judgment calls you make during a listen pass. They are **decisions locked before cleanup starts**.

## When to use clean vs full verbatim

**Choose clean verbatim when:**  
- Client wants readable prose (marketing, podcasts, most freelance jobs)  
- Style guide says “remove filler words” or “clean read”  
- You are optimizing for **clean transcription** deliverables  

**Choose full verbatim when:**  
- Legal, insurance, or compliance transcripts  
- Client PDF says “no omission” or “strict verbatim”  
- Every disfluency may matter for deposition or research

## FAQ

**What is clean verbatim transcription?**  
Transcription that preserves meaning while removing fillers and false starts unless they carry legal or narrative weight.

**Is clean verbatim the same as intelligent verbatim?**  
Same idea, different client names. Always match the **client PDF**, not the label.
