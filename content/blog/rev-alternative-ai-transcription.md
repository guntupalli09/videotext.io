## Why teams switch from Rev

1. **Per-minute pricing** — weekly YouTube channels burn budget on Rev AI + human upsells  
2. **Turnaround** — human Rev is same-day/next-day; AI competitors are minutes  
3. **Output gap** — Rev transcript stops; you still write chapters and SRT manually  
4. **Freelancer workflow** — Rev *graders* care about style rules; Rev *the product* does not format for GoTranscript/Rev freelancer handoff
