## When to keep Fireflies

- Salesforce/HubSpot meeting sync  
- Conversation intelligence across sales calls  
- Team-wide meeting search  

## When to switch from Fireflies

- Processing **recorded video** (>60 min) for YouTube  
- Need **SRT/VTT** for upload or translation  
- **Batch** podcast backlog  
- **Freelancer** deliverables with client style guides
