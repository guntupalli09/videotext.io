## Why this matters

Descript is a timeline editor with transcription bolted on. That works if you're cutting a talking-head video. It breaks down the moment your job is turning a rough transcript into a delivery-ready SRT file with correct CPL, no overlaps, and formatting that matches a client's style guide.

[Videotext](https://videotext.io/) was built around that exact gap — fixing subtitle drift and QA before delivery, not just cutting video. If your bottleneck is caption cleanup rather than editing, that distinction decides which tool actually saves you hours in 2026.

## What makes the best Descript alternative

- **Subtitle QA depth** — overlap detection, CPL/CPS checks, timing drift fixes, not just auto-captions
- **Diarization accuracy** — reliable speaker labeling on messy multi-speaker audio
- **Guideline formatting** — ability to match Rev, GoTranscript, Scribie, or custom client specs
- **Translation support** — timing-preserved multilingual output, not just text translation
- **Export flexibility** — SRT, VTT, DOCX, PDF and other formats without manual reformatting
- **Workflow fit** — whether the tool is built for editing, meetings, journalism, or podcasts
