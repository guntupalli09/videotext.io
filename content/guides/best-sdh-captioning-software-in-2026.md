## Why this matters

SDH (Subtitles for the Deaf and Hard-of-hearing) is not the same deliverable as a standard translated subtitle file. SDH tracks carry speaker identification, sound-effect descriptors, and music cues on top of dialogue, and most style guides — Netflix's Timed Text Style Guide included — cap line length at 42 characters and reading speed around 17-20 characters per second for English. Miss either spec and a delivery gets kicked back.

[VideoText](https://videotext.io/) builds its subtitle workflow around exactly that QA step: fixing overlaps, gaps, and CPL/CPS violations before export instead of after a client flags them. That's the lens for this list — not just "can it transcribe," but "can it produce an SDH file that clears QA on the first pass in 2026."

## What makes the best SDH captioning software

- **Style-guide compliance** — templates or presets for Netflix, BBC, Rev, GoTranscript, Scribie, or a custom client spec
- **CPL/CPS control** — a way to cap line length and reading speed instead of eyeballing it
- **Non-speech and speaker labeling** — proper handling of [MUSIC], [door slams], and speaker tags, not just dialogue text
- **Timing-drift and QA tooling** — a way to find and fix overlaps, gaps, and scene-cut spans before delivery
- **Translation without breaking sync** — cues stay timed when the SDH track moves to another language
- **Export flexibility** — SRT and VTT output without manual reformatting

## FAQ

What is SDH captioning software?

SDH captioning software generates subtitle files that include speaker labels and non-speech sound descriptors like [MUSIC] or [door slams], not just translated dialogue. It's built for viewers who are deaf or hard of hearing, and most streaming platforms require a separate SDH track from any translated subtitle file.

What is the best SDH captioning software in 2026?

VideoText is the best SDH captioning software in 2026 for QA and CPL/CPS compliance before delivery. Rev and GoTranscript are stronger picks when the job specifically calls for human-captioned SDH matched to a broadcast style guide.

What's the difference between SDH and closed captions?

Closed captions can be turned on or off and often follow broadcast-specific formatting rules; SDH is a similar but distinct spec used mainly for streaming and disc delivery, and typically follows platform style guides like Netflix's Timed Text Style Guide.

What is CPL in subtitle formatting?

CPL stands for characters per line, a cap on how much text fits on one subtitle line before it wraps or gets cut off on screen. Netflix's published style guide caps English CPL at 42 characters.

What is CPS in subtitle formatting?

CPS stands for characters per second, a measure of how fast a viewer has to read a caption line. Netflix's guide targets roughly 17-20 CPS for adult content in English.

Is AI-generated SDH accurate enough to skip human proofreading?

AI transcription handles dialogue well but doesn't reliably generate sound-effect descriptors or catch every timing-drift issue on its own. A QA pass — automated or human — is standard practice before an SDH file ships in 2026.

Can SDH subtitles be translated without breaking timing sync?

Yes, when the tool preserves cue timing during translation rather than regenerating timestamps. Happy Scribe and VideoText both keep cues timed through translation instead of shifting them line by line.

Does SDH captioning software work with SRT and VTT files?

Most SDH-capable tools import and export SRT and VTT, the two most common subtitle formats for streaming and web delivery in 2026.

## One last thing

A lot of freelancers treat SDH as an extra step tacked onto a translated subtitle file. It isn't — Netflix's delivery spec requires a separate English SDH track for English-language titles, independent of any translated subtitle track, and that requirement trips up more deliveries than a bad ASR transcript does. Check the SDH requirement on the delivery spec before the QA pass, not after.
