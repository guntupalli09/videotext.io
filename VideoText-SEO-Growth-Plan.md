## Situation Diagnosis

Before the page list — here is what the data is actually telling you:

**You are not in a content problem. You are in a trust problem.**

| Signal | What It Means |
|---|---|
| 14–18% CTR at position 20 | Your titles/descriptions are excellent. Traffic is a ranking problem only. |
| 261 impressions / 3 months | Google has seen your pages but is barely surfacing them. Sandbox. |
| 280+ pages built, ~261 impressions | Volume of pages is not the bottleneck right now. |
| Position moving 52 → 26 → 19 | Google is actively testing and promoting you — healthy signal. |

**Root cause: Google sandbox.** Your site is 3–6 months old. Google withholds trust from new domains regardless of content quality. The sandbox typically lifts between months 4–8. Your 14–18% CTR tells you the machine is working perfectly — you just need Google to show more people the door.

**What actually moves you through the sandbox:**
1. Real backlinks from real sites (the #1 lever)
2. Real user engagement signals (time on page, return visits)
3. Crawl budget health (no thin/duplicate traps)
4. Site authority signals accumulating over time

Everything below is built around this reality.

## Part 4 — Technical SEO (What to Fix This Week)

### Critical — Do First

**1. Schema Markup Audit**
- Every tool page should have `SoftwareApplication` schema
- Every FAQ section should have `FAQPage` schema (verify it is rendering correctly)
- Competitor alternative pages should have `WebPage` + `FAQPage`
- Test at: `https://search.google.com/test/rich-results`

**2. Crawl Budget Health**
You have 280+ pages. Google crawls new sites slowly. Verify:
- Sitemap covers all indexable pages (confirm `sitemap-core.xml` is submitted in GSC)
- No `noindex` tags on pages that should rank
- `robots.txt` does not accidentally block `/assets/` or JS chunks required for rendering

**3. Canonical Tags**
You have many intentional near-duplicate keyword variants:
- `/youtube-to-transcript` vs `/transcribe-youtube-video` vs `/youtube-transcript`

Each must have a `<link rel="canonical">` pointing to itself (not cross-canonicalling to another variant). If variants are cross-canonicalled, Google consolidates them and only ranks one.

**4. Page Speed / Core Web Vitals**
Run PageSpeed Insights on your top 5 pages. LCP under 2.5s is required for competitive rankings. Common issues on React/Vite SPAs:
- Lazy-loaded route chunks taking too long on first render
- Large hero images without explicit `width`/`height` attributes
- Google Fonts blocking render (use `display=swap`)

**5. Index Coverage Report**
In GSC: Coverage → Excluded. Look for:
- **"Discovered, not indexed"** — Google found it but did not crawl (crawl budget issue or low-priority signal)
- **"Crawled, not indexed"** — Google crawled but rejected (thin content signal — fix content depth first)

If you see "Crawled, not indexed" on your programmatic pages, those 2-FAQ pages are the cause. Fix content depth before building more pages.

### Secondary — Do This Month

**6. Breadcrumb JSON-LD** — You have breadcrumb data in `seoMeta.ts`. Ensure it outputs as structured data, not just visual HTML.

**7. Hreflang tags** — When language-specific pages go live (`/spanish-transcription`, `/hindi-transcription` etc.), add appropriate hreflang tags.

**8. Image alt text** — Any screenshots, tool previews, or interface images must have descriptive alt attributes.

**9. Open Graph tags** — Ensure all pages have `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:image`. These affect click-through from social sharing which feeds engagement signals back to Google.
